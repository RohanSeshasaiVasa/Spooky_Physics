# Spooky Physics

A top-down horror physics based game made in godot.

> **Warning: ** This game contains jumpscares.

## Why I made this:
  I had made this initially to support my studies along the way building games and submitting to #terra.

## How to play:

### Controls:

  WASD / Arrow keys - walk

  E - interact with a chore

  Enter - submit your answer

  Esc - step back from a puzzle

### Goals:

  Your final goal is to complete every chore and beat the game.

## How it works:

  So, when you are trying to do a chore, you need to enter the correct answer to the question asked. If your answer is > than the correct answer you get a jumspcare and if it is < the correct answer then you get another chance.
